# Research Brief to Post Workflow

## Workflow

1. Define audience and platform.
2. Collect facts and mark source freshness.
3. Create outline: hook, context, key points, implication, next step.
4. Write draft in the target voice.
5. Fact-check names, dates, numbers, claims.
6. Remove copied phrasing and rewrite originally.
7. Add publish checklist: title, summary, tags, images, citations.
