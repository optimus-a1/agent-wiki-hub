# Evidence Quality And Bias

## Purpose
Define stable research concepts for judging evidence quality, uncertainty, and bias.

## When to use
Use when summarizing papers, comparing claims, or evaluating whether evidence supports a conclusion.

## Stable knowledge points
- KP-01: Evidence quality depends on method, data, measurement, analysis, and transparency.
- KP-02: Internal validity asks whether the study supports its own causal or descriptive claim.
- KP-03: External validity asks whether findings transfer to other settings, populations, or tasks.
- KP-04: Selection bias appears when sampled items differ systematically from the target population.
- KP-05: Measurement bias appears when instruments or labels distort the observed variable.
- KP-06: Confounding appears when another factor explains the observed relationship.
- KP-07: Publication and reporting bias can make available evidence look stronger than the full evidence base.
- KP-08: Uncertainty should be stated as part of the answer, not hidden behind confident prose.

## Core rules
- Separate what a source shows from what it merely suggests.
- Do not fabricate citations, datasets, authors, or results.
- Identify limitations and alternative explanations.
- Avoid claiming consensus from a small or narrow evidence base.

## Workflow
1. Identify claim, evidence type, method, data, and comparison group.
2. Check validity, bias, uncertainty, and reproducibility signals.
3. Compare claims across sources when available.
4. State what is supported, unsupported, and unknown.
5. Recommend further review when evidence is incomplete.

## Edge cases
- A large sample can still be biased.
- A precise estimate can still answer the wrong question.
- A negative result can be informative when method quality is strong.

## Validation checks
- Claims are traceable to provided sources.
- Limitations are included.
- No current literature status is asserted without evidence.
- Citations are not invented.

## Source notes
Stable research methodology only. No current papers, rankings, datasets, or benchmark results are included.
