# Literature Review Workflow

## Workflow

1. Define research question.
2. Search primary sources and record query/date.
3. Cluster papers by method, dataset, claim, limitation.
4. Extract comparable fields: task, data, method, result, limitation.
5. Identify agreement, disagreement, gaps, and future work.
6. Cite sources and avoid overclaiming.
