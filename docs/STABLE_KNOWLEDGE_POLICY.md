# Stable Knowledge Policy

Stable knowledge means long-lived concepts, methods, review patterns, risk boundaries, and workflow structure. It may be model-synthesized when clearly labeled.

Stable knowledge must not include current prices, current versions, current laws, current policies, current platform rules, current vulnerability status, project status, medical guidance, legal conclusions, or verified evidence claims.

Changing facts must remain in source review until authoritative, dated evidence and required human gates pass.
